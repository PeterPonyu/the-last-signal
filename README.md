# The Last Signal

An original golden fantasy browser adventure starring Zeyu, Dawnweaver and Sol.

[Play the game](https://peterponyu.github.io/the-last-signal/)

Free browser play, with no account or installation. Five connected maps (the Sunken Garden, Echo Causeway, Hollow Sun Court, Emberfoundry and Starfall Observatory) offer defeat-driven story progression, two branching roads each with a protected keeper mission, a roaming champion and a final Warden: ten named boss encounters, fifteen named keepers and fifteen regional enemy types alongside the common Hollow. A full journey takes roughly 35–45 minutes at the default game speed; duration depends on difficulty, route, upgrades and retries. Fight with automatic ranged light and Sunblade, and learn Dawn Pulse, Sunbreak and Solar Lance from the answers of the light; Dawnweaver learns Lunar Nova and Sol gains Comet Volley. Every figure is painted raster artwork with distinct anticipation, impact, recovery and fallen poses. Automatic combat powers are enabled by default and can be disabled in pause settings. Escort and repair NPCs fight and have health; if captured, they return to their starting road after three seconds, and pressing F there restarts that mission. Dawnweaver heals nearby allies, Sol wards them, and E shields active keepers.

The game fills the screen on phones, tablets and computers without scrolling, in landscape or portrait, and is available in English and Simplified Chinese (switch on the title screen or in the pause menu). Keyboard and mouse: WASD or arrows move, J or left click swings Sunblade, Space dashes, E releases Dawn Pulse, Q Sunbreak, R or right click Solar Lance, F talks, chooses a road or enters a gate, Escape or P pauses. Touch: drag on the left to move and tap the action buttons; a context button appears near keepers, roads and gates. Gamepad (standard layout): left stick or d-pad moves, A Sunblade, B dash, X Dawn Pulse, Y Sunbreak, RB or RT Solar Lance, LB interact, Start pauses; menus work with the d-pad, A and B. Ranged light fires automatically, and instructions can be hidden.

This repository contains the deployable game, including its JavaScript runtime source in app/ and content/. Serve the directory through any static HTTP server to play locally. Open index.html through HTTP rather than directly from the filesystem, because the game loads ES modules and an asset catalog.

The main character and companion are illustrated fictional adaptations. Character bodies, environment paintings and radiance atlases are original raster artwork: generated for the first three maps; the Emberfoundry and Starfall Observatory enemies and bosses and the scenery of all five maps were drawn in code and then repainted locally with Stable Diffusion 3.5 Medium, the late-map enemies and the Furnace Sovereign with a house-style LoRA trained only on the game's own paintings (Stability AI Community License; Powered by Stability AI). Clear danger outlines, interface symbols and lightweight particles are rendered in code. Reference photographs and private development records are not included. The original live score is synthesized in the browser and is off until enabled.

The game runs locally in the browser. Last results and instruction preferences use browser storage. Active journeys and cloud saves are not supported. The wider six-chapter story remains future content.
